01 / ABOUT US

A Trusted
Healthcare Destination
in Tadepalligudem

Ravi Madhav Nursing Home, also known as Ravi Madhav Hospital, serves patients in Subbaraopeta and surrounding areas with dedicated medical care, accessible support and a patient-first approach.

The hospital is located on Sheshamahal Road in Karri Satyavathi Nagar, Subbaraopeta, Tadepalligudem. This website brings together the information a patient needs before a visit — doctors, departments, services, patient areas, directions and direct enquiry options.

before you travel.

If a detail is not listed here, call Ravi Madhav Nursing Home directly so the hospital can confirm the current information.

How can I contact the hospital?

Call +91 95815 33399. The alternative mobile number supplied is +91 94907 45291.

Where is Ravi Madhav Nursing Home?

Sheshamahal Road, Karri Satyavathi Nagar, Subbaraopeta, Tadepalligudem – 534101, Andhra Pradesh.

Can I send an appointment enquiry online?

Yes. The enquiry form prepares a WhatsApp message that you can review and send from your device.

Can I rely on the website for consultation timings?

Timings and doctor availability can change. Please call the hospital to confirm before visiting.

Which doctors are listed?
